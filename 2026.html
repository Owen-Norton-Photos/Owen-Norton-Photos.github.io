2026

In 2026 I began to treat photography as slightly more than a hobby, and began to use it as an artistic outlet. I submitted to several art shows, and began to put my work on display around my community. I try now to capture specific things, and treat the rest of the image as an extension of the focus. The spontaneity of photography as opposed to other visual fine arts is one of its more impactful characteristics to me, and no images below were posed or planned.
